image (1024×1024 PNG).

Built for the make.fun bounty: [Sum up 25 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125).

## What it covers

- **Lead:** Ukraine hits a Russian cargo ship with a drone strike in the
  Gulf of Finland; ally Norway urges caution. Ukraine also sank a Russian
  naval vessel in a Libyan port.
- **Mines hit three tankers in Hormuz:** Iran's Revolutionary Guard Navy
  says three oil tankers were hit by naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Nvidia slides as tariff fears return:** Nvidia fell 6-7% a day after
  surging 31.5%; the Nasdaq lost 1.5% and the S&P 500 0.5%.
- **Gaza strike kills Hamas security chief:** an Israeli airstrike in
  western Gaza City killed Naeem Abu Nahel, director of Hamas' internal
  security apparatus.
- **Pakistan's first astronaut in orbit:** Muhammad Zeeshan Ali launched
  aboard China's Shenzhou-24 to the Tiangong station.
- **Hasina to return home on 16 December:** ousted prime minister Sheikh
  Hasina plans to return from exile in India on Victory Day.
- **Houthis hit Riyadh's tallest tower:** a Houthi strike wounded 11;
  Saudi airstrikes killed 17 in Hodeida and 26 in Lahj.
- **Malaysia to send 6,500 Rohingya back in December:** days after the
  World Court ruled Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya.

Also: England beat Australia by eight wickets in the second T20I; GTA 6
out worldwide since 19 November; a 5.6 quake hit Nepal; AIDS deaths fell
to 570,000, the lowest in 20+ years; Sarkozy's corruption case goes to
trial.

All headlines were verified against real news sources on 25 November 2026.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This renders the front page and saves it as `entry.png` next to `main.py`
(and also copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` when that variable
is set). The program is deterministic: running it twice produces
identical output bytes. It uses only the Python standard library and no
network access. The typeface and the hero illustration are drawn by the
program itself.
